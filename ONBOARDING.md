# SIWES Connect Pilot — Onboarding Kit (1 department, 15–20 companies)

## For companies (send this 1-pager on WhatsApp/email)

**Get verified IT interns in 3 steps — free during pilot.**
1. Sign up at [APP-URL]/signup as **Company**, complete your profile (company name, CAC number, contact, website + CAC photo). Approval within 24 hours.
2. Post an opening: **Quick post** (1 minute) or **Custom** (your own screening questions).
3. Review applicants per opening → **Shortlist / Accept / Reject**. Shortlisted students get your contact from their dashboard — interviews happen on phone/WhatsApp as usual.

Rules: real openings only. Fake postings = suspension + delisting.

## For students (post in class groups)

**Real, verified SIWES placements — no more word-of-mouth.**
1. Sign up at [APP-URL]/signup as **Student**, complete your profile (name, department, level, CGPA, skills, CV + school ID/admission letter). Approval within 24 hours.
2. Open **Openings** — your best matches are ranked first with reasons (“2/3 skills match”). Filters: department, location, skill, duration.
3. Apply (max **5 active** at a time — withdraw dead ones to free slots). Track every status in **My applications** with days-pending.
4. After placement: rate the company so the next set stays safe. Bad experience? **Report this company** on any posting — reviewed within 48 hours.

Never pay anyone for a placement. Report accounts that ask for money.

## For you (admin) — verification checklist

**Student approval (2 min each):**
- [ ] Name matches the school ID photo
- [ ] Department is one of the 35 SIWES-approved options
- [ ] Level is 400 (or 500 spillover)
- [ ] Admission letter / ID is readable, not blurry
- [ ] CV opens and looks like the same person
- Reject (with reason, e.g. “ID photo blurry — re-upload”) if anything fails.

**Company approval (5 min each):**
- [ ] CAC number format valid (RC…); spot-check on the CAC public search when in doubt
- [ ] Phone number rings / WhatsApp responds; ask what role the intern will do
- [ ] Website or social page exists and matches the company name
- [ ] Prefer companies with a history of taking interns (your 15–20 warm list first)

**Daily ops:** clear verification queue + report queue (48h SLA) → check Trust metrics → suspend on confirmed scam (auto-freezes all their openings).
